# Pet before travel (fictional): question checklist

Apply the general checks in `../README.md` first.

**Plan (already answered, so not to be asked):** a two-year-old rescue dog, adopted two weeks
before a seven-day work trip, staying at the sister's house, and she has agreed to look after
it.

| ID | Perspective | Finding (short) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `e1-f1` | Health & Safety | Vaccination status matters if the sister's household has other animals |
| `e1-f2` | Health & Safety | Unknown health or medication needs |
| `e2-f1` | Human & Behavioral | Two weeks may be short to settle before another move |
| `e2-f2` | Human & Behavioral | Separation-related behaviour is unknown |
| `e3-f1` | Operations & Logistics | Daytime supervision at the sister's house |
| `e3-f2` | Operations & Logistics | Getting the dog to and from the sister's house |

## Case-specific checks

- [ ] **Vaccination and other animals aren't bundled.** "Does your sister have other animals"
      and "which vaccinations has the dog had" can get different answers. If the finding is
      asked about, it's one of these (the other animals question arguably decides whether
      vaccination matters at all), or both as separate questions.
- [ ] **No question about where the dog will stay** or whether the sister has agreed.
- [ ] **Justified consolidation.** A question about how long the dog will be alone at the
      sister's house could reasonably cite both `e2-f2` (separation behaviour) and `e3-f1`
      (daytime supervision), since the same answer bears on both. Citing `e2-f1` too needs
      a reason.
- [ ] **Priority.** Several valid sets exist. Transport (`e3-f2`) is a weaker choice than the
      other animals, time alone, or health needs, but isn't wrong if well justified.

## Examples (illustrative, not required wording)

Acceptable:
- "Does anyone else in your sister's household have animals the dog would share space with?"
- "On a typical day during the trip, how long would the dog be alone at your sister's house?"
- "Has the shelter told you about any ongoing health conditions or medication the dog needs?"

Bundled (fail):
- "Does anyone else in your sister's household have animals, and if so, are they
  vaccinated?" (a conditional follow-up: ask the prerequisite on its own)
- "Does your sister have other pets, and what vaccinations has the dog had?" (two topics)
- "How will the dog get to your sister's, and will it be alone during the day?" (two topics)
- A `why_it_matters` of "…; please also confirm whether the dog is microchipped" (a hidden
  request)
