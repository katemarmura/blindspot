# Small business (fictional): question checklist

Apply the general checks in `../README.md` first.

**Plan (already answered, so not to be asked):** a small bakery in a rented storefront, opening
in three months, funded from savings, with two part-time staff.

| ID | Perspective | Finding (short) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `e1-f1` | Financial | Savings runway before revenue is steady |
| `e1-f2` | Financial | Equipment costs may exceed the setup budget |
| (e2) | Legal & Regulatory | **Failed**: missing coverage, no findings |
| `e3-f1` | Operations & Logistics | Two part-time staff may not cover opening hours |
| `e3-f2` | Operations & Logistics | Lease terms may limit fit-out work |

## Case-specific checks

- [ ] **No invented legal questions.** No question about licences, food-hygiene
      registration, or permits: the Legal & Regulatory perspective failed, and questions
      must come from supplied findings. No question cites an `e2-*` ID (none exist).
- [ ] **No question about how the bakery is funded or how many staff there are.**
- [ ] **The runway question is one question.** A single question such as "how many months
      of fixed costs could your savings cover?" is fine: one answer, which can inform both
      `e1-f1` and `e1-f2`. Asking for the savings amount **and** the rent **and** the
      expected revenue in one question is bundled.
- [ ] **Equipment and lease stay separate if both are asked.** Whether the storefront is
      already equipped (`e1-f2`) and whether the lease allows alterations (`e3-f2`) can get
      different answers.
- [ ] **Priority.** With four findings and at most three questions, a set covering runway,
      equipment or lease, and opening hours is reasonable; other justified choices can
      pass.

## Examples (illustrative, not required wording)

Acceptable:
- "Roughly how many months of rent, wages, and other fixed costs could your savings cover
  with no sales?" (one quantity; the examples define "fixed costs")
- "Is the storefront already fitted with commercial baking equipment?"
- "What opening days and hours are you planning?"

Bundled or out of scope (fail):
- "How much have you saved, what is the rent, and what revenue do you expect?"
- "Does the lease allow alterations, and is the storefront already equipped?"
- "Have you obtained the necessary food licences?" (no supporting finding, since the legal
  perspective failed)
